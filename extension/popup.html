Downloads

    Video is always saved as MP4: it plays everywhere and holds the subtitles, chapters and cover. For Music, pick the file type:

    FormatPick it whenKeep in mind
    M4A defaultYou want the best sound: it’s YouTube’s own audio (AAC), not converted. Phones, computers, VLC and modern cars play it.Some old car stereos and MP3 players can’t open it.
    MP3It has to play on anything, or a program or site only takes MP3.Converted from the original, so a little quality is lost; bigger file, and it takes a moment more.
    OpusYou want the original in the smallest file: YouTube’s Opus audio, not converted (a .webm file).No cover or song details inside. Browsers, VLC and Android play it; Apple devices and many players don’t.
    WAVYou’ll edit it in an audio editor or DAW.Uncompressed: about 10 times bigger, with no better sound than M4A. No cover inside.

    Logged-in downloads reach age-restricted and members-only videos, but heavy downloading with an account can get it flagged.
